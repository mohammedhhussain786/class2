Part A — Aptitude

Simplification

Simplification means solving a mathematical expression by following the correct order of operations to reach the simplest possible answer.

10
+
5
×
2
=
20

Not 30 — multiplication must be performed before addition.

RULEBODMAS

The most important concept in simplification. Work through the expression in this order:

BBrackets( ), { }, [ ] — innermost first
OOrdersPowers, roots
DDivisionSame priority as Multiplication — left to right
MMultiplicationSame priority as Division — left to right
AAdditionSame priority as Subtraction — left to right
SSubtractionSame priority as Addition — left to right
Division and multiplication share priority — so do addition and subtraction. Students often wrongly assume multiplication always comes before division; both are correct only when solved strictly left to right.

BTypes of brackets

( )

Square bracket

Solve from the innermost bracket outward:

[ 10 + { 5 + (2 × 3) } ]  →  [ 10 + { 5 + 6 } ]  →  [ 10 + 11 ]  →  21

FRFractions

Very common in aptitude exams — be comfortable adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing them.

Addition

1/2 + 1/3 → LCM=6 → 3/6+2/6 = 5/6

Subtraction

3/4 − 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2

Multiplication

2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5

Division

2/3 ÷ 4/5 → invert & multiply → 2/3×5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6
